Eqlio

Corporate app for sports challenges: it makes wellness activities accessible and engaging for every employee.

Role
Product / UX/UI Designer
Format
UX/UI concept
Platforms
iOS, Web

Brief

Create a mobile app for participants and a web dashboard for administrators. The system had to motivate people at very different fitness levels and make the competition fairer.

My role

Research, product logic, user flows, UX/UI design and an interactive prototype.

Benchmark analysis

People want to take part in sports challenges – without feeling they have already lost

For the benchmark analysis I compared YuMuuv, Pacer for Teams, Wellable and MoveSpring, focusing on challenge mechanics, the fairness of leaderboards and the tools available to administrators.

Unequal starting points

Absolute numbers favour the already fit and quickly discourage newcomers.

Motivation drops

If the goal looks unreachable, interest fades within the first few days.

No overview

HR teams struggle to see participation at a glance and to spot who needs extra support.

Hypothesis and direction

Competition motivates when progress feels personal and the terms feel fair

If the result is measured against a participant’s own starting point, more employees will stay in the challenge regardless of fitness level.

Personal progress

Goals and progress are calculated against the starting level, not only against absolute numbers.

Team support

Shared challenges, a team result and support from colleagues help keep it regular.

Transparent management

The administrator sees engagement, progress and problem areas in one dashboard.
