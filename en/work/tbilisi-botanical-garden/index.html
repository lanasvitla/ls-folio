01 Goal

Create a mobile guide to Tbilisi Botanical Garden that helps visitors choose a route and find their way during a walk.

02 Problem solving

The guide brings the garden map, place information and walk planning together. Visitors can compare time, terrain and steps before choosing a route, then adjust their walk, find a rest stop or head to an exit.

•• 14Key interactions

Explore your way

Four ways to turn a garden visit into a walk that fits you.

  1. Find a place

    Search for a landmark, a plant or an exit. Move from a result to its details without losing your context.

  2. Make time for the garden

    Choose how long you have and the kind of path you prefer before you start.

  3. Explore on the map

    See places in their surroundings. Switch the map view and keep your bearings.

  4. Adjust your walk

    Pause to check the terrain ahead. Continue, choose another path or find an exit.
